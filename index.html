WHAT TO EXPECT

What to Expect at Your Fitting

Every garment crafted at OPULENCE & HEIRLOOM begins with individualized pattern drafting and meticulous attention to drape, comfort, and silhouette.

01

Accurate Body Measurements

Detailed measurements taken by our master tailor to ensure proper shoulder balance, comfortable armhole ease, and a tailored fit.

02

Textile & Silhouette Advisory

Bring your own fabrics, heirloom lengths, or unstitched suits. We evaluate drape, grain, weight, and lining structure before scissors touch fabric. (Pure bespoke tailoring — clients provide their chosen textiles.)

03

Trial & Basting Fit Checks

Try on your garment before final finishing to inspect fit, neckline depth, sitting comfort, and fall with our master tailor.

04

Direct WhatsApp Coordination

Immediate booking confirmations, milestone fitting alerts, and garment delivery notifications sent directly to your phone.
